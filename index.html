Our Heritage

Five Decades of Mathematical Excellence

Founded in 1980 under the guidance of Rajan Jagtap, Jagtap Classes has stood as a premier educational institute in Solapur. For nearly half a century, we have simplified complex mathematical concepts and prepared generations of students for academic success.

As we march toward our 50-year milestone (1980–2030), our Solapur campus continues to foster problem-solving skills, analytical rigor, and core conceptual clarity.
